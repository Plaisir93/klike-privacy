Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Klike pa ranmase okenn enfòmasyon sou ou. Li travay nèt san entènèt. Pa gen kont pou louvri, pa gen reklam, pa gen anyen k ap swiv sa ou fè.

Sa ki rete sou aparèy ou

Tout sa Klike kenbe rete nan aparèy ou. Li pa janm soti, epi nou menm nou pa janm wè l:

  • Leson ou fini yo ak pratik ou fè nan Laboratwa a.
  • Konbyen tan ou pase sou chak leson.
  • Chwa ou yo: lang nan, vwa k ap li a, jan kisè a mache.
  • Non ou ekri pou setifika a, si ou chwazi mete youn.
  • Yon nimewo aza pou aparèy la. Non ou pa ladan l.

Sa Klike pa fè

  • Li pa voye anyen sou entènèt.
  • Li pa mande yon kont, yon imèl, oswa yon modpas.
  • Li pa gen reklam.
  • Li pa gen zouti pou mezire oswa swiv sa ou fè.
  • Li pa pataje anyen ak okenn lòt konpayi.

Pèmisyon

Klike mande pèmisyon pou jwe son sèlman, pou vwa ki li leson yo. Li pa janm itilize mikwo ou, kamera ou, kote ou ye, kontak ou, oswa foto ou.

Timoun

Klike pa ranmase anyen sou pèsonn, kidonk li pa ranmase anyen sou timoun non plis. Nenpòt moun, kèlkeswa laj li, ka sèvi ak li.

Sa ou ka fè

  • Chanje oswa retire non ou: ale sou paj setifika a epi chanje l.
  • Efase pwogrè ou: ale nan Paramèt, epi klike sou «Efase pwogrè mwen».
  • Retire tout bagay: dezenstale aplikasyon an. Tout sa ki te sou aparèy la ale nèt.

Magazen yo

Lè ou telechaje Klike nan Google Play oswa nan App Store, se magazen an ki ba ou li, epi se règ pa yo ki mache pou telechajman an. Klike pa resevwa okenn enfòmasyon nan men yo.

Chanjman

Si règ sa a chanje, n ap mete nouvo dat la anwo paj la.

Klike collects no information about you. It works entirely offline. There is no account to create, no advertising, and nothing that tracks what you do.

What stays on your device

Everything Klike keeps stays on your device. It never leaves it, and we never see it:

  • The lessons you have finished and the practice you have done in the Laboratwa.
  • How long you spend on each lesson.
  • Your settings: the language, the narration voice, how the pointer behaves.
  • The name you type for your certificate, if you choose to enter one.
  • A random identifier for the device. It does not contain your name.

What Klike does not do

  • It sends nothing over the internet.
  • It does not ask for an account, an email address, or a password.
  • It carries no advertising.
  • It contains no analytics or tracking tools.
  • It shares nothing with any other company.

Permissions

Klike asks only for permission to play audio, for the voice that reads the lessons aloud. It never uses your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photos.

Children

Klike collects nothing about anyone, so it collects nothing about children either. Anyone, at any age, can use it.

What you can do

  • Change or remove your name: go to the certificate page and edit it.
  • Erase your progress: go to Paramèt (Settings) and choose “Efase pwogrè mwen”.
  • Remove everything: uninstall the app. Everything stored on the device goes with it.

The app stores

When you download Klike from Google Play or the App Store, the store delivers it to you and its own privacy policy governs that download. Klike receives no information from them.
